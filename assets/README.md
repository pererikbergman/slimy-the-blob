# Assets

Everything in this folder is copied next to the game's executable and into its distribution zip,
except this README. The game loads files from here with `app.assets`.

`ship.png` (128 by 128 texels, pointing up) is generated art from the
[klyph repository](https://github.com/inemtsev/klyph), where it is the player ship of the sample
shooter in `games/shooter/assets/`. It was made with xAI Grok Imagine and keyed out from a solid
background. Replace it with your own art.
